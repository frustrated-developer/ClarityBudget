Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026 · ClarityBudget

The short version

ClarityBudget stores your financial records in private app storage on your device. The app does not send your transactions, account names, balances, budgets, categories or notes to ClarityBudget. Android Auto Backup may transfer the database, receipts and selected preferences to your Google account if device backup is enabled; this is a platform-managed backup, not ClarityBudget cloud sync. We have no ClarityBudget servers or user accounts.

Optional usage analytics and crash reporting are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings → Privacy & Data, they help us find bugs and understand which features are used. These events do not include transaction amounts, notes, account names or category names. You can turn collection off again in Settings.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter:

  • accounts and their balances
  • transactions, including amounts, dates and notes
  • receipt photos you attach, stored privately on your device
  • budgets and their limits
  • categories you create
  • your preferences: currency, language and theme

This lives in a private database on your device that only ClarityBudget can read. The app does not transmit these records to ClarityBudget. If Android Auto Backup is enabled, the operating system may include the database and receipt files in the device backup, as described below.

Receipt photos are copied into ClarityBudget's own private storage rather than referenced from your gallery, so a receipt stays attached to its transaction even if you later delete the original photo. They are readable only by this app.

ClarityBudget asks for no camera or photo permissions. Taking a photo hands off to your camera app, and choosing an existing one uses Android's photo picker, which gives this app only the single image you select—never your library.

What we collect

Anonymous usage data (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand which features are used and where people get stuck, we record events such as:

  • which screens are opened
  • that a transaction was saved, updated or deleted, and whether it was income, an expense or a transfer
  • that a budget or account was created, updated or deleted
  • how many transactions were deleted in a bulk action
  • your chosen currency code and language code
  • whether dark mode and biometric lock are switched on
  • that an export or backup was performed

This never includes transaction amounts, notes, account names, category names, balances, or anything else that identifies you or your finances.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the app crashes we receive a report so we can fix it. It contains technical details of the failure—the error, device model, operating system version and app version—plus a short trail of the screens visited and kinds of actions taken beforehand (for example, "Screen: Transactions" or "Transaction saved: EXPENSE"). No amounts, names or notes are included.

Turning it off

Go to Settings → Privacy & Data → Share anonymous usage data. This is off when the app is first installed. Switching it on enables usage analytics and crash reporting; switching it off stops both immediately. Data already sent before you opt out cannot be recalled.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or any third party beyond the Google services described above.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not ask you to create an account or provide an email address.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, or the contents of your device.

Backups

Android Auto Backup

Android may back up ClarityBudget's database, receipts and selected preferences to your Google account so they can be restored if you lose or replace your phone. This is managed by Android and your device's backup settings; you can disable it there. ClarityBudget does not operate or have access to this backup.

Android documents end-to-end encryption for cloud backups on Android 9 and later when a screen lock is set and device backup is enabled. Protection can differ on older Android versions or when those conditions are not met. ClarityBudget does not encrypt the Android backup itself. We exclude Firebase installation data from the paths configured for backup.

Because ClarityBudget has no cloud sync, this is the only thing that protects your records if your device is lost—which is why it is left switched on.

Backups and exports you create yourself

When you use Back up data or Export, the resulting file is written where you choose—for example, your Downloads folder. Those files are yours and are not protected by the app. Anyone with access to them can read their contents, so take the same care with them as you would with a bank statement.

Deleting your data

Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete all data permanently erases every account, transaction, budget, category and receipt photo on the device, and resets your preferences. This cannot be undone.

Two things it does not reach:

  • Files you exported yourself. Backup ZIP and PDF files you saved to Downloads are left alone because you chose to put them there. Delete them yourself if you want them gone.
  • Anonymous data already sent. Usage and crash data transmitted before deletion cannot be recalled. It contains nothing that identifies you.

Uninstalling the app also removes its local data, though an Android Auto Backup may still exist in your Google account until you delete it there.

Children

ClarityBudget is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this policy and its "last updated" date before the change ships.
